Help & Support

Visits & scanning

The QR code won't scan

Make sure the whole code is inside the camera frame and reasonably lit. If the business has just rotated its verification word, ask them to display the current code — Proof Chain rejects codes signed with an expired word.

My visit wasn't recorded

Visits are written to your device first, so they appear even without a connection. If a visit is missing, check that you completed the scan through to the confirmation screen. Repeated scans at the same business within the rate-limit window are intentionally rejected.

Do I need an account to use Proof Chain?

No. Customers never give a name, email address or phone number. Your visit history is kept on your device, and cloud backup stays off unless you turn it on. Two things do leave your device: every check-in is verified on our server to stop forged visits, and if you choose an alias at a business, that business can see the visits you made there. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what is sent. Accounts exist only for business owners.

Business accounts

I can't sign in

Confirm your email address is verified — check your inbox for the confirmation link, including the spam folder. If you signed up with Apple or Google, use that same button rather than a password. You can request a fresh password reset link from the sign-in screen at any time.

The password reset link doesn't open the app

Open the link on the iPhone or iPad where Proof Chain is installed, and make sure the app is up to date. Tapping the link from a desktop browser will show a confirmation page but cannot hand the session back to the app.

Subscriptions

Restoring a purchase

Open the app, go to the subscription screen and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to the Apple Account that bought them, so use the same one.

Managing or cancelling

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. Manage or cancel them in the Settings app under your name → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the renewal; access continues until the end of the paid period.

Still stuck?

Email hi@proofapp.site and include your device model, iOS version and the app version from Settings. We read every message.
